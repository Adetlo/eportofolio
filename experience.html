Bank Negara Indonesia · Head Office

General BankingBranch OJTCore Banking ISTMulti-division Rotation

A one-year structured programme for graduates entering the IT track, combining general banking education, on-the-job training in a branch and in-service training inside the IT division.

  • General banking: products, deposits and lending, treasury, trade finance, remittance, accounting, AML and KYC, risk, audit and the OJK and Bank Indonesia regulatory environment

  • Core banking induction: the AIX and COBOL platform, daily batch operations, the Oracle database layer and the integration messaging to channels and downstream systems
  • Rotations across application development, database and data warehouse, infrastructure, IT security and IT project management
  • Final placement in the Core Banking division, promoted to Assistant Manager
