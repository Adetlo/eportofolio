Officer Development Program (IT)

PT Bank Negara Indonesia (Persero) Tbk — Head Office

A one-year structured officer-development program for high-potential graduates entering the IT track. The curriculum combined General Banking education, On-the-Job Training (OJT) at a branch, and In-Service Training (IST) inside the IT division — designed to produce officers who understand both how a bank operates commercially and how its technology underpins those operations.

  • General Banking education (classroom). Foundations of Indonesian banking and BNI's business model — banking products and services, deposits and lending, treasury, trade finance, remittance, accounting principles, anti-money-laundering / KYC, risk & compliance, audit, and the regulatory environment (OJK, Bank Indonesia). Also covered leadership, business communication, and BNI's corporate culture.
  • On-the-Job Training (OJT) at a branch. Placed inside an operating branch to learn end-to-end branch processes from the customer-facing side: teller and customer-service operations, account opening, payments and clearing, cash handling, branch back-office reconciliation, loan origination workflow, and the day-to-day controls used by branch staff. The goal was to internalise how a branch actually serves customers and what IT systems each role depends on.
  • In-Service Training (IST) in the IT Division — Core Banking. Placed inside the Core Banking division at Head Office for hands-on technical induction: the IBM AIX / COBOL core platform, daily batch operations, the database layer (Oracle), and the integration messaging that connects core to channels and downstream systems. Worked alongside senior engineers on real change requests and incident handling.
  • Cross-divisional IT exposure. Shorter rotations across the wider IT organisation — Application Development (channels, internet/mobile banking), Database & Data Warehouse, IT Infrastructure (data centre, network, servers), IT Security, and IT Project Management — to map how the divisions hand off to each other.
  • Final placement at the end of the program: Core Banking division, promoted to Assistant Manager.
