Profile

From core banking code to leading a bank's technology

I joined Bank Negara Indonesia in 2015 through its IT Officer Development Program and spent the next seven years in the core banking division at head office, first as an engineer and then managing a core banking team. That work ran on IBM AIX, COBOL and Oracle: daily batch, posting and general-ledger flows, and the messaging that connects the core to channels and downstream systems. Delivery included the core banking implementation for the New York branch, data segregation for the BNI Syariah to BSI merger, and national payment schemes such as BI-FAST, QRIS and Virtual Account.

In 2022 I moved to the London branch, first as IT Supervisor and since 2024 as Head of IT, accountable for the branch's application landscape, infrastructure, cybersecurity and its technology obligations under the PRA and FCA. Since January 2026 I have also served as Acting Head of Accounting, which keeps me close to the ledger, the financial controls and the reporting that the systems exist to serve.

I still write code. Branch automation, reconciliation and reporting tools are built in-house, and I am extending that engineering into data, AI, identity and security: an MSc in Data Science from the University of Essex, completed in 2026, certifications in Python, Oracle SQL and PL/SQL, Microsoft Entra identity and Oracle AI, and a public engineering portfolio on GitHub built on synthetic data and generic architectures.
