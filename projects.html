Payments & market infrastructure

SWIFT, national real-time schemes and merchant collections

ISO 20022 CBPR+ migration, London Branch

Project lead, branch sideLondon BranchSWIFT · Payments

Challenge

SWIFT's cross-border migration from legacy MT messages to ISO 20022 MX, while back-office systems at the branch still only accept MT.

Approach

Drove branch readiness: operations awareness, training and end-to-end testing. On the SWIFT bureau, implemented MT⇄MX translation so legacy systems keep operating during and after the transition.

Technology & domain

  • ISO 20022 / CBPR+
  • SWIFT MT → MX
  • SWIFT bureau
  • Payments
  • Treasury

Outcome

Payment, treasury and back-office staff work confidently in the new standard, with no data loss or reconciliation breaks through the migration.

How I work

Three habits behind the case studies

01

Build for the audit

Every system above posts to a ledger someone will reconcile and a regulator may inspect. Audit trails, exception handling and reconciliation are designed in from the start, not added later.

02

Bridge enterprise and modern

COBOL on AIX and Python in a container both have a place in a bank. Knowing both sides makes migrations and integrations safer and keeps modernisation grounded in how the core actually behaves.

03

Ship with the business

Compliance officers, treasurers and branch staff shaped each of these tools. Working alongside them, and now running accounting as well as IT, keeps the engineering tied to real operational needs.
